# English Glossary for the Yooli Case

This glossary standardizes recurring terms in the English mirror of `yooli-doc`. Many of these expressions have specific meanings in China’s P2P regulatory and wind-down context and should not be translated mechanically.

| Chinese term | Preferred English rendering | Usage note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 出借人 | **lender** | Preferred over “investor” when describing the legal P2P lending relationship. “Investor” may be used only when discussing general user perception or investment behavior. |
| 借款人 | **borrower** | The underlying debtor under a loan contract. |
| 借贷余额 | **outstanding loan balance** | Usually unpaid principal still outstanding under P2P loan contracts. Do not equate automatically with a liability of the platform operating company. |
| 待收本金 | **principal receivable** / **outstanding principal** | Choose according to the source wording and context. |
| 待收 | **receivable balance** / **outstanding receivables** | May include more than principal in some user-facing contexts; do not assume a single definition. |
| 代偿 | **guarantee compensation** / **third-party compensation** | Payment by a guarantor or other third party because the borrower failed to perform. “Compensation” here does not mean damages in the tort-law sense. |
| 累计代偿金额 | **cumulative compensation amount** | Historical cumulative amount; not a point-in-time outstanding loss. |
| 债权 | **claim** / **creditor’s claim** | “Claim” is generally preferred in the English mirror. |
| 债权转让 | **claim transfer** / **assignment of claim** | Transfer of a lender’s creditor rights to another holder. |
| 债权受让人 | **claim assignee** | Person or entity acquiring the claim. |
| 债权认领 | **debt-claim recognition** / **claim recognition** | Yooli wind-down mechanism under which lenders were assigned or substituted into identified underlying claims. It is not cash repayment. |
| 债权置换 | **claim substitution** | Exchange of one claim pool or account claim for another underlying claim. |
| 属地化债权认领 | **locality-based debt-claim recognition** | Yooli’s late-stage scheme for matching lenders with claims in selected localities. |
| 清退 | **wind-down and resolution** | Preferred to “liquidation.” In China’s P2P context, 清退 can include collection, balance reduction, claim transfers, debt substitution, and operational exit without corporate liquidation. |
| 清退完成 | **completion of the wind-down** | Does **not** imply that lenders recovered 100% of principal. Operational/regulatory exit and lender recovery are separate questions. |
| 良退 / 良性退出 | **orderly exit** when referring to official policy language; **full-principal resolution** when referring to this project’s strict lender-recovery concept | Important distinction. In regulatory documents, 良性退出 is usually translated as “orderly exit.” In this project’s lender-side analytical usage, “良退” is used strictly for a resolution in which remaining lenders recover **100% of principal**. Do not silently equate the two. |
| 逃废债 | **malicious debt evasion** / **strategic debt evasion** | Regulatory term for deliberate nonperformance or evasion; it must not be applied to every borrower who defaults because of genuine inability to pay. |
| 备案 | **regulatory filing** / **filing registration** | Not a “license.” P2P filing expectations were distinct from bank or financial-institution licensing. |
| 监管试点 | **regulatory pilot** | Distinct from information-disclosure or product-registration pilots. |
| 信息披露试点 | **information-disclosure pilot** | A self-regulatory / industry-infrastructure pilot; not proof that formal P2P filing had been completed. |
| 产品登记试点 | **product-registration pilot** | Again, not equivalent to formal regulatory filing. |
| 实时数据报送 | **real-time regulatory data reporting** | Connection to a data-reporting system does not itself constitute regulatory approval of the platform. |
| 定存宝 | **Dingcunbao** | Product name retained in pinyin. Its “service period” could differ from the maturity of underlying loans, and exit often depended on claim transfer. |
| 服务期 | **service period** | Product-level period used by Dingcunbao; not necessarily the maturity term of the underlying claim. |
| 底标 | **underlying loan** / **underlying claim** | Choose according to whether the focus is the borrower loan contract or the creditor right held by the lender. |
| 底层资产 | **underlying assets** | The loan claims and related credit assets behind platform products. |
| 底层逾期率 | **underlying-project overdue rate** / **underlying delinquency rate** | Preserve the source’s definition where available. |
| 逾期 | **overdue** / **delinquent** | “Overdue amount/project” for platform metrics; “delinquent borrower/loan” for credit-performance discussion. |
| 严重失信借款人 | **serious-default borrower** | Project shorthand for Yooli’s published category. It should not be read as a generic legal status equivalent to every court “dishonest judgment debtor” designation. |
| 坏账 | **bad debt** | Use where the source itself used a bad-debt concept. Do not substitute “bad debt” for every overdue claim. |
| 本息全回 | **full principal-and-interest recovery** | Means the lender recovered both principal and interest under the platform’s wording. Whether the figure was independently audited must be stated separately. |
| 本息保障 | **principal-and-interest protection** | For historical Yooli marketing. Where the source expressly says 担保, “principal-and-interest guarantee” may be appropriate. |
| 刚兑 | **rigid repayment guarantee** / **implicit guaranteed-repayment expectation** | China-specific concept describing an expectation that investors/lenders will be made whole regardless of underlying asset performance. |
| 担保 | **guarantee** | Usually legal guarantee / surety. Distinguish from generic marketing “protection.” |
| 担保方 / 担保公司 | **guarantor / financing-guarantee company** | A financing-guarantee company may be licensed; confirm status from source before describing it as licensed. |
| 回购 | **repurchase** | Often an asset partner’s contractual obligation to buy back a claim or asset after specified events. |
| 追偿权 | **recovery right** | Right acquired by a guarantor or compensating party to seek reimbursement from the borrower after compensation. |
| 资管公司 | **asset-management company** | Generic term unless licensing status is independently established. |
| 第三方AMC | **third-party asset-management company / third-party AMC** | In this project, shorthand for the anonymous Yooli wind-down counterparty. It does **not** imply a licensed national financial AMC or local AMC unless separately proven. |
| 实缴资本 | **paid-in capital** | Capital actually contributed. It is not the same as registered capital and not necessarily equal to available cash. |
| 认缴资本 | **subscribed capital** | Shareholder commitment to contribute capital under the company’s registered arrangements. |
| 注册资本 | **registered capital** | Registered corporate capital; not proof that the same amount of cash was actually paid in or remained available. |
| 资金池 | **fund pool** | A pool in which funds are aggregated rather than matched and segregated as required. The term carries legal/regulatory implications and should not be used without evidence. |
| 自融 | **self-financing** | Financing the platform itself, its controllers/shareholders, or related parties with lender funds. Deep asset-side integration alone does not establish self-financing. |
| 债转 | **claim transfer** | Common shorthand for 债权转让. |
| 债转流动性 | **claim-transfer liquidity** | Availability of assignees willing to acquire old claims, enabling existing lenders to exit. |
| 三降 | **“three reductions”** | Policy shorthand for reducing platform/institution count, business scale, and participants; exact components can vary by policy document and level, so preserve source context. |
| 压降 | **balance reduction / scale reduction** | Regulatory and operational reduction of outstanding P2P exposure and participant numbers. |
| 存量 | **existing stock / outstanding stock** | Existing P2P loans, lenders, or exposures remaining to be resolved. |
| 存量化解 | **resolution of existing stock** | Process of reducing and disposing of remaining P2P exposures. |
| 信息中介 | **information intermediary** | The formal regulatory positioning of a P2P platform under China’s online-lending rules. |
| 增信 | **credit enhancement** | Guarantees, repurchase, collateral, compensation, or other arrangements increasing expected repayment support. |
| 风险准备金 | **risk reserve** | Use only where the source specifically refers to a reserve; do not infer one from generic guarantee arrangements. |
| 垫付 | **advance payment** | Payment made before formal compensation or settlement by another party. Do not equate automatically with platform self-funding. |
| 资金桥 | **funding bridge** | Analytical term in this project for the funding source that may have maintained lender payments between borrower default and later formal guarantor compensation. It is not an official regulatory term. |
| 兑付 | **repayment / payout** | Context-dependent. For a P2P lender, “repayment” is preferable when money comes from the borrower/guarantor; “payout” may be used when describing user experience or resolution outcomes. |
| 爆雷 | **platform failure / crisis becoming public** | Colloquial Chinese term. Avoid literal translation in formal English. |
| 踩踏 | **run / disorderly rush to exit** | Usually refers to a self-reinforcing rush by lenders to withdraw or transfer claims, potentially worsening liquidity stress. |

## Translation principles

1. **Preserve evidentiary strength.** “涉嫌” should remain “suspected / may be suspected,” “当事人称” should remain “the party alleged/stated,” and a jurisdiction or procedural ruling must not be rewritten as a merits finding.
2. **Do not collapse different metrics.** “Outstanding loan balance,” “principal receivable,” “overdue amount,” “cumulative compensation,” and “notice-appearance accumulated overdue amount” are not interchangeable.
3. **Do not convert registered capital into cash.** Registered, subscribed, paid-in, and currently available funds are distinct concepts.
4. **Do not call every asset-management counterparty an AMC in the regulated financial sense.** Unless licensing is established, use the descriptive generic term.
5. **Keep official policy language separate from this project’s analytical vocabulary.** In particular, official “orderly exit” does not mean that lenders necessarily recovered 100% of principal.
6. **Preserve Chinese case numbers and original source links.** English case titles are descriptive aids and are not official translations.
