# English Documentation

This directory is the research-oriented English mirror of the Chinese documents in [`docs/`](../docs/). It is intended to make the Yooli case materials more accessible to international scholars, legal researchers, financial-regulation researchers, and others studying China’s P2P wind-down.

**The Chinese originals are authoritative.** These English files are research translations, not official or certified legal translations. If any wording, legal nuance, company name, policy title, or evidentiary strength differs, the corresponding Chinese document controls.

Company and policy names are translated descriptively on first use where helpful, while Chinese names, case numbers, dates, figures, and source links are preserved as far as practicable. Terms specific to China’s P2P regulatory and wind-down context are standardized in the [English Glossary](./GLOSSARY.md).

## Research and citation note

The project deliberately separates confirmed facts, platform statements, media reports, litigant allegations, and analytical inferences. Statements such as “may,” “possible,” “not established,” “party allegation,” or “no merits finding” are intentional evidentiary qualifiers and should not be removed when citing or reusing the English materials.

For litigation or formal legal use, researchers should consult the Chinese originals and, where possible, the underlying Chinese court decisions, regulatory documents, contracts, archived pages, and audit reports rather than relying only on these translations.
